# Theme settings

Open **Online Store → Themes → Customize → Theme settings**. Settings apply across the store unless a section has its own controls.

## Brand

- **Display brand name** optionally replaces the displayed store name in the header, footer, password page, and social site name. It does not change your Shopify store name or SEO page titles.
- **Favicon** is the small browser icon. Use a clear square image.

## Typography

Set the font, letter spacing, base size, and line height for **Headings** and **Body text**. **Small labels** control compact metadata and control labels. **Navigation** controls the font and size used by menus.

Change typography in small increments, then check long product titles, form fields, menus, and mobile layouts. Avoid using display fonts for body text where readability suffers.

## Collection tiles and product cards

**Collection tiles** control the image source, image ratio, title font/size, and text treatment for collection cards. **Product cards** control image fit, vendor visibility, sale and sold-out badges, second image on hover, title styling, and price styling.

Use **Contain** image fit when it is important to show the entire product. Use **Cover** when a consistent editorial crop is more important. Preview both products with and without images.

## Cart and search

- **Cart type** chooses the Cart drawer or Cart page as the normal add-to-cart destination.
- **Cart icon** chooses the header icon.
- **Show order note** enables the customer order note.
- **Checkout note** is an informational note; Shopify still calculates taxes and shipping at checkout.
- **Search behavior** opens the full search page or the search drawer.
- **Predictive search content** limits suggestions to products or includes products and content.

## Colors and buttons

Theme settings include colors for general surfaces, interactive states, the header, announcement bar, footer, overlays, and commerce prices/badges. Keep text and background colors readable in every combination.

**Button CTA** controls button colors, type, sizing, padding, border width, and corner radius. **Form fields** control the minimum height, border width, and corner radius of standard fields.

## Social media

Add complete URLs only for social networks your store actively uses. Empty fields do not create a social link.

## Safe editing checklist

After any global setting change, preview a product, a collection, the cart, search, and the footer at Compact (under 48rem), Tablet (48–63.99rem), and Desktop (64rem and above) widths.
