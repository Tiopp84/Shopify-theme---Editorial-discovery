# Custom Liquid and app blocks

## Custom Liquid

Narrivelle provides a **Custom Liquid** section for section-enabled storefront templates and a **Custom Liquid** Product block in the main Product section.

To add it:

1. Open the target template in **Customize**.
2. Select **Add section** and choose **Custom Liquid**, or add the Product block on a product template.
3. Enter your Liquid in the provided setting.
4. Preview the page on desktop and mobile before saving.

Blank Custom Liquid does not create storefront output. Use it for small, self-contained enhancements, not to recreate a theme feature or override checkout, customer accounts, payments, pickup, or Shopify provider data.

## App blocks

The main Product section and Featured product support app blocks where Shopify makes an installed app block available. Add the app block from the section's **Add block** menu, then configure it according to that app's instructions.

Narrivelle cannot guarantee an individual app's availability, performance, accessibility, or support policy. Test each app block with the app provider before publishing and after theme updates.

## Safe custom-code practice

- Duplicate the theme before adding custom Liquid.
- Add one change at a time and record what it does.
- Do not paste unknown scripts from tutorials into the theme.
- Keep checkout, payment, accounts, pickup, and app/provider-native data under Shopify or the relevant provider's ownership.
- If a custom change breaks layout, remove it from the duplicate theme before contacting support.
