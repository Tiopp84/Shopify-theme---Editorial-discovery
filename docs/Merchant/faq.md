# Frequently asked questions

## Can I change my store name with Narrivelle?

The optional **Display brand name** changes selected storefront display locations only. Change your official Shopify store name in Shopify admin.

## Why is a payment, pickup, or Follow on Shop surface missing?

These are Shopify/provider-controlled features. Their availability depends on store configuration, market, customer/device context, product/variant data, and provider eligibility. Confirm the relevant Shopify setup before changing the theme.

## Can I change checkout?

No. Checkout and payment methods are owned by Shopify. Use Shopify's supported checkout settings and apps rather than Custom Liquid or theme code.

## Why does my product section have an empty area in the editor?

Some sections show editor-only placeholders when no product, collection, image, or block content has been selected. Choose real content, then preview the storefront. Empty optional content is normally omitted from the live page.

## How do I add an FAQ or contact page?

Create a Shopify Page, select the `page.faq` or `page.contact` template, then open that page in the theme editor to add or edit its supported content.

## Can I add custom code?

Use Custom Liquid only for small, tested enhancements. Duplicate your theme first. Customizations and third-party app conflicts are outside standard bug-fix support unless a separate agreement says otherwise.

## How do I report a problem?

Use the public support form when it is published. Include your store URL, Narrivelle version, affected page URL, steps to reproduce, expected and actual behavior, and screenshots or a recording.
