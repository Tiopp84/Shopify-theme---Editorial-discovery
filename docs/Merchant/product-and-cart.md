# Product and cart

## Product page

Select a product in Shopify admin, then use the theme editor's product template to arrange supported Product blocks. The main Product section can include blocks for:

- Vendor, title, price, SKU, availability, and description
- Product form and purchase controls
- Collapsible content and reassurance content
- Pickup availability and payment terms
- Custom Liquid and app blocks

Add, remove, or reorder only the blocks that are available in the Product section. Keep a product form on the page so customers can select a variant and add it to the cart.

For the full Product block and section inventory, see the [section reference](section-reference.md).

## Product data

Manage titles, prices, compare-at prices, variants, images, media, inventory, selling plans, and product descriptions in Shopify admin. Narrivelle reads that data; it does not create variants, calculate inventory, or determine an item's eligibility for payment or pickup.

For the best product page, provide accurate variant options, alt text, product media, and a clear description. Test products with long titles, unavailable variants, and missing media before launch.

## Native Shopify payment and pickup surfaces

The dynamic checkout button, payment terms, pickup availability, Follow on Shop button, and Apple Wallet/Gift Card behavior are provider- or Shopify-owned. They can appear, update, or be unavailable according to your store configuration and the customer's context. Do not replace them with manual copy or branded lookalikes.

## Related products

Use the Product recommendations section to configure related or complementary product intent where Shopify supplies recommendations. If no recommendations are available, the section may not display storefront content.

## Cart

Narrivelle supports the Cart drawer and Cart page. Customers can change quantity, remove items, add an order note when enabled, and continue to Shopify checkout. Shopify validates the final cart, discounts, taxes, shipping, selling plans, and payment methods.

If your store uses tax-inclusive pricing, Shopify supplies the state used to show the tax-inclusive notice. Do not manually add a conflicting tax message.

## Before publishing

Test a product with multiple variants, a sold-out or unavailable option, a sale price, and a cart with more than one item. Check the Cart drawer and Cart page at compact and desktop widths.
