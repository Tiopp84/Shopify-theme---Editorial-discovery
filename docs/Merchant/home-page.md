# Home page

Open **Online Store → Themes → Customize**, then choose **Home page** from the page selector. Use **Add section** to add available home-page sections, drag sections to reorder them, and use the eye icon to hide a section without deleting its settings.

## Recommended editing order

1. Set the editorial hero and its primary destination.
2. Add product or collection-led merchandising sections.
3. Add editorial story sections only where they support a product decision.
4. Preview the complete page before adding more sections.

## Available home-page compositions

Narrivelle includes editorial sections such as **Editorial hero**, **Featured edit**, **Material & craft**, **Shoppable hero**, **Shoppable story**, **Pinned visual story**, and **Outfit composition**. It also includes supporting content such as **Email signup**, **Scrolling text**, **Featured product**, **Custom Liquid**, and collection-led sections.

Each section owns its own content, layout, and style controls. Use real headings, images, links, and products. Empty product/resource selections may show an editor placeholder but should not be treated as final storefront content.

For every available section and its supported controls, see the [section reference](section-reference.md).

## Product-led sections

Use a collection or product picker whenever a section provides one. Product title, price, availability, variants, and purchase behavior continue to come from Shopify. Do not put product facts into an image or duplicate a product price in manual copy.

**Featured product** supports native Product blocks and app blocks. Configure the selected product first, then use its section settings to adjust media position, media width, thumbnails, and other available presentation controls.

## Editorial sections

Use image alt text in Shopify wherever it is available. Keep headings meaningful without relying on the image. Check sections with a missing image, a long heading, and a long button label before publishing.

## Header and footer

The Announcement bar and Header are edited in the **Header** section group. Footer content is edited in the **Footer** section group. They are global: a change applies across the storefront.

Do not use page-body sections to recreate global navigation, account, cart, or checkout controls.
